2
How Your Data Flows

Generating a letter

  1. Your reflection text, first name, selected emotion, and the Bible verse are sent over HTTPS to our Firebase Cloud Function (Google, United States).
  2. The server runs a one-time crisis keyword check. If crisis language is detected, it stops and shows crisis resources — your text is not sent to Anthropic.
  3. Otherwise the data is forwarded to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the letter.
  4. Anthropic returns the letter to our server, which returns it to your device. We do not store your reflection text or the generated letter in our database.
  5. The letter is saved on your device only if you tap Save.

Using the Say chat

  1. Each message you send is saved to our database (Firestore) under your account, and sent — along with recent messages for context — to Anthropic to generate a reply.
  2. The AI reply is saved to our database too, so you can return to the conversation later.
  3. Periodically, an AI-generated summary of a prior day's conversation may be created and stored to support a gentle "check-up" opener and reach-out reminders (see §4).

Unlike letters, Say conversations are stored on our servers until you delete the chat or your account.

4
The Say Chat & Reach-Out Reminders
The "voices" in Say are AI, not real people. Say is an AI chat powered by Anthropic's Claude. It is not a counselor, therapist, or human listener.

Storage. Your Say messages and the AI's replies are stored in our database (Firestore) under your account so conversations persist across sessions. Recent messages are sent to Anthropic with each new message to provide context.

Reach-out reminders. If you enable notifications, the app may send a gentle "reach-out" or a next-day check-up opener that is generated from a summary of your prior Say conversation. You can mute specific voices or turn off notifications entirely in Settings.

Your controls. You can delete an individual chat, mute reach-outs per voice, disable notifications, or delete your entire account (which removes all Say data from our servers — see §12).

5
Local Storage — What Stays on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device (AsyncStorage). We cannot read or retrieve it:

  • Emotion check-in history
  • Saved Bible verses
  • Letters you choose to save, and their reflection text
  • Onboarding profile answers (faith background, life stage, intent) and age-gate result
  • App preferences (theme, notifications, haptics, crash-reporting choice)
  • Your subscription tier, mirrored locally for fast UI

This local data is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Start over in Settings.

6
Push Notifications

If you enable reminders, your device OS (Apple APNs or Google FCM) generates a push token. We store that token together with your timezone offset and reminder/mute preferences in our database (Firestore) so we can deliver reminders and reach-outs at an appropriate local time. You can disable notifications at any time in Settings or your device settings; disabling them stops delivery, and account deletion removes the stored token.

7
Crash & Error Reporting

To diagnose crashes and bugs, the app can send diagnostic reports to Sentry. These reports include error messages, stack traces, device/OS information, and the sequence of actions leading to an error. We configure the app to avoid sending the content of your letters or Say messages in these reports, but diagnostic data can be sensitive.

Your choice. You can turn crash reporting off in Settings → Crash reporting. When turned off, the app does not send diagnostic reports to Sentry.

8
Crisis Keyword Detection
This is not a crisis monitoring service. The app does not monitor you in real time or employ human reviewers.

When you submit text to generate a letter or send a Say message, our server runs a one-time automated check for crisis-related language. If detected, the app shows crisis resources (such as 988 and the Crisis Text Line) instead of a normal response. This is a safety aid only — not counseling, monitoring, or intervention. If you are in danger, contact emergency services or a qualified professional immediately.

Letters and Say replies are generated by Anthropic's Claude AI. AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, or theologically imprecise, and is not reviewed by clergy or professionals. It is not advice of any kind. See our Terms of Service for the full AI-content disclaimer.

Data Retention & Deletion

Account & Say data (our servers)

Your Firebase account data and your Say conversations are retained until you delete them or delete your account. You can delete an individual Say chat in the app. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) runs a server process that removes your Say conversations, push token, usage records, and Firebase Authentication account, and clears local data on your device. We keep a minimal, non-content deletion audit record for compliance.

Anthropic-processed data

Text sent to Anthropic is subject to Anthropic's retention policies, which we do not control. See anthropic.com/privacy.

RevenueCat & platform records

Subscription records are retained by RevenueCat and the app stores per their policies and tax law.

Crash reports

If crash reporting is on, Sentry retains diagnostic data per its retention schedule.

Local device data

Deleted immediately when you uninstall or use Start over; we cannot recover it.

Security

  • Authentication is handled by Google Firebase.
  • Database access is restricted by security rules so that each user can read only their own data; conversation writes are performed by our server.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
